FIELD MANUAL

Move A/D or arrows · jump W · backflip Backspace. Aim with the pointer or Q/E; hold Space or FIRE to charge, release to shoot. Tab switches worms before you act.

Camera. It frames your worm and the nearest enemy by itself. T (🎯 TARGET) picks the next enemy; a click on an edge arrow picks that worm. Mouse wheel or Z (🗺 VIEW) shows the whole map, right-drag looks around, C snaps back. Aim anywhere in view, even zoomed out.

Ninja rope (8): fires along the aim; A/D swing, W/S reel, Space lets go. Teleport (9): click a free spot, two per match. Neither ends your turn.

Weapons. Shotgun gives two shots; other attacks end the turn. Grenade fuse 1–5 s, high or low bounce. Airstrike: click the target, either side. Jetpack: four seconds, X drops dynamite.

Map. Mines blow a second after a worm comes close. Barrels explode and chain. Health crates (+25) parachute in; walk into them. Water is deadly and rises after round 8.
